# Kenney audio (CC0)

Picked from three packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), all CC0 1.0 (public domain; credit not
required, given in the game's credits anyway):

- Interface Sounds 1.0 (the clicks, opens, errors, confirmations), from the Godot packaging at
  github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds, converted from wav to ogg.
- RPG Audio (coins, book pages, cloth), from github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-rpg-audio-for-godot.
- Impact Sounds (wood, soft, metal, glass, footsteps), from
  github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot.
