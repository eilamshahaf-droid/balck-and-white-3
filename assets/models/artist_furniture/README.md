# Artist furniture

Modelled for this game in Blender (own work, CC0), by `tools/blender/build_furniture.py`.
Units are Kenney raw units, origin at the middle of the footprint on the floor, front toward +z,
so they sit beside the Kenney Furniture Kit in the room designer. Each GLB embeds its textures
(a 256 px wood image or a 256 px leaf card with alpha); icons are in `icons/`.
Plants are crossed leaf cards (alpha), not solid shapes.
